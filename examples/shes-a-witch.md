# She's a Witch!

A village brings Sir Bedevere a witch. Bedevere, wise in the ways of science, settles it by strict logical reasoning. Follow along, one step at a time.

Scene transcript: https://www.qedcat.com/moviemath/holy_grail.html

{time=15 reveal=counts}

- [x] She looks like one
- [x] She's got a wart
- [x] She turned me into a newt! (I got better.)
- [x] They dressed me up like this!

## How do we tell whether she is made of wood?
{scoring=scale}

- [x] See whether she floats in water
- [0.25] Build a bridge out of her (but bridges are also made of stone)
- [0.5] Throw her into the pond and see if she sinks
- [ ] Ask her

## What also floats in water?
{points=1500}

- [x] A duck!
- [ ] Very small rocks
- [ ] Churches
- [ ] Lead

## So, logically, if she weighs the same as a duck…

- [x] …she's made of wood, and therefore a witch!
- [ ] …she's a duck
- [ ] …the scales are broken
- [ ] …we need a bigger duck

## Bedevere argues: wood floats, and things that weigh the same as a duck float, so she is wood. Which fallacy is that?
{points=2000 time=40}

- [x] Affirming the consequent
- [x] Undistributed middle
- [ ] Ad hominem
- [ ] None: it's sound. Burn her!
